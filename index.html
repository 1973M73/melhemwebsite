Original Research Initiative

Governance Psychology™

Governance Psychology™ is an original research initiative founded by Melhem Salloum. It examines how behavioral factors influence governance effectiveness, ethical conduct, organizational trust, and enterprise risk decision-making.

The initiative integrates governance structures, behavioral science, risk management, organizational trust, and ethical decision-making into practical frameworks for understanding organizational performance and control behavior.
